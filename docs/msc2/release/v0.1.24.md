# MSC 2 v0.1.24

Publication requested by Cameron through the existing build-only beta workflow.

Add a top-left Biomes panel with a color toggle and clickable legend, a bottom-right Quality panel with live presets and sliders, and independent vertical Depth controls in 2D, 3D and Fly. Depth shows standard Overworld sea level; Alt/Option+Up/Down adjusts depth with held-key repeat, and Alt/Option+Left/Right changes the 1/4/16-block keyboard step. Shift retains Fly descent. Generic modded block shapes remain unchanged. Bedrock biome display depends on exported biome data.

Retain the complete headless helper bundle including bedrock-map and Vantage, checksums and signed update metadata. No tests run, no workflow gates added. Physical acceptance remains owner verification.
