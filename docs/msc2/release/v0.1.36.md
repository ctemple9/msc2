# MSC 2 v0.1.36

This candidate carries the app changes in the
[v0.1.33 candidate notes](v0.1.33.md) and the bundled-helper signing fix in the
[v0.1.34 candidate notes](v0.1.34.md). Earlier candidates v0.1.33 through
v0.1.35 were not published.

Apple accepted both v0.1.35 macOS apps. The Intel app passed signature and
Gatekeeper checks inside the finished DMG, but its app-level stapled ticket did
not survive disk-image packaging. This release notarizes and staples the final
signed DMG on both architectures, then validates that DMG ticket and the app
inside the mounted image. This follows Apple's recommendation to staple the
outermost distribution container.

The build-only workflow still requires all nine platform artifacts, SHA-256
checksums, and signed update metadata before publication. Cameron's clean-Mac
Finder first-open check remains open until he installs this exact download.
